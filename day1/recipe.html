Recipe: Bread Sandwich

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • Butter
  • Cheese
  • Vegetables
  • Sliced chicken
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional Items/Substitutes:
    • Mayonnaise instead of butter
    • Avocado slices as a substitute for cheese
    • Grilled vegetables as an alternative to raw vegetables
    • Additional seasonings like garlic powder or paprika for extra flavor

Steps

  1. Spread 1 tbsp of butter on one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Preheat a pan over medium heat.
  3. Cook for 2-3 min until the bread is golden brown.
  4. Cook the chicken with 1 tbsp of oil for 10-15 min
  5. Grill the vegetables for 2-3 min until they are tender.
  6. Layer cheese, vegetables, and sliced chicken on one slice.
  7. Season with half a tsp of salt and pepper.
  8. Place the other slice on top to create a sandwich.

Cooking terms glossary

Butter
A dairy product made from churning cream, used for cooking and baking.
Cheese
A dairy product made from curdled milk, available in various types and flavors.
Vegetables
Edible plants or parts of plants, such as lettuce, tomatoes, and cucumbers.
Sliced chicken
Chicken meat that has been cut into thin pieces for easy layering in the sandwich.
Salt and pepper
Common seasonings used to enhance the flavor of food.
